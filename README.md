### Projects

#### [MediAngel](https://github.com/rashedt2/mediangel) — smart pill dispenser
A four-slot ESP32 dispenser, a FastAPI backend that owns the schedule, and a
Flutter app for the caregiver. The device holds no logic of its own: it polls
the backend for commands and reports results back, so the schedule has one
source of truth and the hardware can be power-cycled mid-dose without losing or
duplicating one.

Built as a graduation project with a team — the original repository is
[zuhairsalhi/MediAngel](https://github.com/zuhairsalhi/MediAngel).

`Flutter` · `FastAPI` · `SQLAlchemy` · `ESP32` · `SQLite`

#### [Wedding Invitation](https://github.com/rashedt2/wedding-invitation)
A digital wedding invitation that opens as a sealed envelope rendered in WebGL.
Arabic-first with an English mode, and a full non-WebGL fallback so it still
works on a device that can't render the entrance.

`Next.js` · `React Three Fiber` · `TypeScript` · `GSAP` · `Tailwind`

#### [Restaurant Landing Page](https://github.com/rashedt2/restaurant-landing-page)
A cinematic Arabic RTL single-pager for a restaurant. Motion is opt-out
everywhere, video degrades to a poster image, and the intro can't trap your
scroll.

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `GSAP` · `Lenis` · `Tailwind`

#### [Coffee Roastery Site](https://github.com/rashedt2/coffee-roastery-site)
A 120-frame scroll-scrubbed canvas hero, in plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with
no dependencies. The frame shown is a pure function of scroll position, so the
motion is always exactly where you left it.

`Vanilla JS` · `Canvas` · `CSS`
