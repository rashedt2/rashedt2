### Projects

#### [MediAngel](https://github.com/rashedt2/mediangel) — smart pill dispenser
A four-slot ESP32 dispenser, a FastAPI backend that owns the schedule, and a
Flutter app for the caregiver. The device holds no logic of its own: it polls
for commands and reports results, so the schedule has exactly one source of
truth and the hardware can be power-cycled mid-dose without losing or
duplicating one.

`Flutter` `FastAPI` `SQLAlchemy` `ESP32` `JWT` `SQLite`

#### [Wedding Invitation](https://github.com/rashedt2/wedding-invitation) — 3D bilingual invitation
A sealed envelope rendered in WebGL that the guest breaks open. Arabic-first
with an English mode, a full non-WebGL fallback path, and RSVP writing to a
Google Sheet. Ships with the design case study that preceded it.

`Next.js 16` `React Three Fiber` `TypeScript` `GSAP` `Tailwind`

#### [Restaurant Landing Page](https://github.com/rashedt2/restaurant-landing-page) — client work
A cinematic Arabic RTL single-pager. Motion is opt-out everywhere, video
degrades to a poster, and the intro can't trap your scroll.

`Next.js 15` `TypeScript` `GSAP` `Lenis` `Tailwind`

#### [Coffee Roastery Site](https://github.com/rashedt2/coffee-roastery-site) — no framework
A 120-frame scroll-scrubbed canvas hero, in plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with
zero dependencies. The frame is a pure function of scroll position, so the
motion is always exactly where you left it.

`Vanilla JS` `Canvas` `CSS` `Design tokens`
